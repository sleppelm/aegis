Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Aegis is a private assistant used by one household on its own home server. It is not offered to the public. This policy describes what Aegis accesses from Google accounts and what it does with it.

What Aegis accesses

  • Family members: only their name and email address, read once from Google when they sign in, to check that the account is on the household's own list. Nothing else in their Google account is accessed.
  • The household owner's own account, with permissions the owner grants:
    • Gmail: reading messages, writing drafts (Aegis never sends email), and labels and archiving;
    • Google Calendar: reading events and adding or changing events;
    • Google Drive: reading files, and creating and editing only the files Aegis itself created;
    • Google Contacts: reading contacts;
    • Google Tasks: reading and adding tasks.

How it is used

Google data is used only to answer the household's own requests (for example "what's on my calendar tomorrow?" or "add the dentist on Friday") and to show them their own information. To answer a request, the relevant text may be processed by an AI language model (Anthropic's Claude, through its API) or by models running on the household's own computers. It is not used to train or improve AI models.

Storage and security

  • Sign-in tokens and the household's data are stored on the household's own server, on its home network. Tokens are kept on the server and never shown in the assistant's conversations.
  • A family member's sign-in is kept only as a one-way fingerprint, and is removed when the owner removes that person.
  • Aegis asks the owner to confirm before anything is deleted, shared or sent to other people.

Sharing

Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone outside the household, except the AI processing described above, which happens only to answer the household's own requests.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Aegis's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Anyone can remove Aegis's access to their Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The owner can also disconnect an account or remove a family member in Aegis's settings, which deletes the stored sign-in.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Aegis.
