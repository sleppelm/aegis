Aegis

A private home assistant for one household.

Aegis is a self-hosted assistant that runs on a single family's own home server. It helps that household with everyday things: their calendar and email, the TVs and lights at home, reminders and questions.

Aegis is not a public service. There is no sign-up, nothing is sold, and only the household's own Google accounts, added by hand, can sign in to it.

Signing in with Google

Members of the household sign in with their Google account. Depending on the account, Aegis asks Google for either just the person's name and email address (family members), or for access to the household owner's own Gmail, Calendar, Drive files, Contacts and Tasks so the assistant can help with them. The privacy policy explains exactly what is accessed and how it is handled.
